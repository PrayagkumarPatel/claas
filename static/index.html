03 REFERENCE — HOW THE BOOK WORKS

One book, two sides. The spread is the only negotiation.

▾ FOR COMPUTE PROVIDERS

  1. List your capacity. Pick a hardware tier, set your price per GPU-hour, size the offer. It hits the book instantly.
  2. Get matched. Crossing buyers fill against you immediately at your price. Otherwise your offer rests as visible liquidity.
  3. Sell every hour. Partial fills mean buyers take exactly what they need — the rest of your offer keeps working.

▴ FOR AI TEAMS

  1. Post a request. Name your price and batch size in GPU-hours. No negotiation, no contracts, no sales calls.
  2. Fill from the top. You're matched with the cheapest supply first, sweeping price levels when you need volume.
  3. Pay for what fills. Your unmatched remainder keeps resting at your price until supply crosses down to it.

04 NOTES

N.01 Is real compute being traded here?

No — and that's deliberate. This MVP is a faithful simulation of the marketplace layer: the order book, matching engine, and price discovery are real code, but the participants and liquidity are bots. A production version adds escrowed payments, identity verification, and the genuinely hard part — orchestrating containerized workloads on provider machines (scheduling, GPU drivers, networking, data transfer, verified disk wipes).

N.02 How is the price determined?

Pure supply and demand on a central limit order book. Bids and asks cross at the maker's price; the spot price is simply the last executed trade. Simulated startup demand follows a time-of-day curve (morning training rush, afternoon inference peak, overnight lull), so prices move in recognizable waves.

N.03 What's a GPU-hour?

One GPU allocated for one hour. 8 GPUs running for 30 minutes = 4 GPU-hours. It's the fungible unit that makes heterogeneous hardware tradeable on one book — an RTX 4090 hour and an H200 hour are priced differently, but both are GPU-hours.

N.04 What does it cost to use?

$0 in this MVP. Production compute marketplaces typically charge a 5–15% take rate on filled volume, plus payment processing (~3%). The order confirmations here show the fee line explicitly so the economics stay visible.

N.05 Do I need an account?

No. This demo has no signup and collects nothing — your tracked orders live in your browser's local storage only. The engine's state is in-memory, so restarting the backend resets the market.

N.06 Why does the price move in waves?

The simulation compresses a 24-hour demand cycle into 3 real minutes so you can watch a full trading day in one sitting. Watch the demand curve strip on the trading floor — when demand peaks, bids get aggressive and the spot price climbs.
